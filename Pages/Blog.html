🌌 Quantum Fields

When I use the term "Quantum Fields", I mean something personal: a connection between mindset, personal development, and the way we experience life.

This journey started after reading books from Dr. Joe Dispenza, who shares ideas about personal transformation, meditation, mindset, and creating positive changes in life.

I explored some of these techniques in my own life and experienced positive changes that inspired me to share my journey and observations.

Note: The following sections describe lifestyle approaches and personal experiences. They are not a replacement for professional medical advice.

🌱 Anti-Depression Diet & Lifestyle

Nutrition and lifestyle choices can influence energy levels, mood, and overall well-being.

Healthy Habits

  • Avoid alcohol and smoking.
  • Instead of three large meals, eat five or six smaller meals.
  • Replace heavy dinners with:
    • Salads
    • Fruits
    • Healthy carbohydrates like boiled potatoes or whole-grain pasta
  • Reduce processed sugars and use natural sources such as:
    • Dates
    • Honey
    • Fruits
  • Drink enough water and maintain proper hydration.
  • Add vegetables daily and include fruits such as apples and oranges.
  • Eat magnesium-rich foods such as spinach and leafy vegetables.
  • Avoid fast foods and highly processed foods.

🧘 Anxiety Disorders & Lifestyle Support

Understanding and addressing the causes behind anxiety is an important part of managing it.

Healthy Habits

  • Avoid smoking and alcohol.
  • Reduce excessive caffeine intake:
    • Coffee
    • Tea
    • Chocolate
  • Include more whole grains (غلات سبوس دار).
  • Stay hydrated.
  • Add more fruits and vegetables.
  • Eat a handful of nuts daily.
  • Include fish regularly.
